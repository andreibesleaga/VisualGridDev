# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participants in this project commit to making participation a respectful
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be evidence-led. Disagree with a claim by addressing the claim, not the author.
- Accept that a specification under review will be challenged. Challenge is the
  process working.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before concluding.
- Respect that this project proposes systems that can affect people, including in
  safety-critical, industrial, and healthcare contexts. Design carelessly and
  someone may build on it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Contributing material intended to enable surveillance, targeting, weapons
  coordination, or harm to people.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub's reporting
mechanisms. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The
maintainer may remove content, close contributions, or restrict participation,
and will give reasons where it is appropriate to do so.

## Attribution

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1, adapted for a specification repository.
